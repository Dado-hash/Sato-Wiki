On October 5, 2025, Bitcoin surpassed $125,000 for the first time, reaching a new all-time high of $125,245. The milestone marked a nearly 80% gain from the $100,000 breakthrough in December 2024 and a new chapter in Bitcoin's price discovery following a year of transformative developments.

![Bitcoin price chart showing the 2025 rally from $100,000 to $125,000.](media/history/bitcoin-ath-2025/bitcoin-2025-price-chart.webp "Bitcoin 2025 price rally")

The rally was built on several converging factors. The U.S. Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, established in March 2025, had signaled formal government recognition of Bitcoin as a strategic asset. Spot Bitcoin ETF inflows continued at a record pace, with institutional investors — pension funds, endowments, and sovereign wealth funds — increasingly allocating portions of their portfolios to Bitcoin. The fourth halving's supply reduction continued to compound, with daily new issuance capped at 450 BTC while institutional demand steadily absorbed available supply.

The macroeconomic backdrop was also supportive. The U.S. dollar weakened amid uncertainty surrounding fiscal policy and government funding debates, driving investors toward alternative stores of value. Bitcoin's fixed supply narrative resonated powerfully in this environment, attracting both retail and institutional buyers seeking protection from currency depreciation. The $125,000 milestone represented not just a numerical record but a confirmation that Bitcoin's institutional integration — set in motion by the ETF approvals and accelerated by sovereign adoption — had fundamentally reshaped its market dynamics.
