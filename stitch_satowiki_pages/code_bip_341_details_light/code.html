Taproot: SegWit version 1 spending rules

Authors Pieter Wuille, Jonas Nick, Anthony Towns
Created 2020-01-19
Layer Consensus

menu_book Plain English Summary

Taproot is a significant upgrade to the Bitcoin protocol designed to improve privacy, efficiency, and flexibility. It introduces a new way of processing transactions that makes complex smart contracts (like multi-signature wallets or Lightning Network channel opens) look identical to standard, single-signature transactions on the blockchain. By obscuring the complexity of the underlying scripts until they are strictly necessary, Taproot ensures that users executing advanced functions do not compromise their privacy or pay exorbitant fees compared to regular users.

Practical Impact

  • check_circle Lower Fees: Multi-sig and complex transactions require less data, reducing on-chain footprint.

Technical Details

Schnorr Signatures (BIP 340) expand_more
Replaces ECDSA. Schnorr signatures are linearly mathematical, allowing multiple signatures and public keys to be aggregated into a single key and signature. This is the foundation for making multi-sig look like a standard transaction.
Merkle Abstract Syntax Trees (MAST) expand_more
Allows complex smart contracts to be broken down into their individual conditions (branches). When spending, only the executed branch needs to be revealed to the blockchain, drastically saving space and preserving the privacy of unexecuted conditions.
Tapscript (BIP 342) expand_more
An update to Bitcoin's Script language specifically for Taproot spending paths. It removes certain size limits, simplifies opcode validation, and paves the way for easier future upgrades to the scripting language via opcodes.
