Bitcoin Pizza Day

May 22, 2010

#Milestone #Commercial

On May 22, 2010, Laszlo Hanyecz made history by completing the first documented commercial transaction using Bitcoin. He paid 10,000 BTC for two Papa John's pizzas, an event now celebrated annually as "Bitcoin Pizza Day."

The Transaction

Hanyecz, an early Bitcoin contributor and programmer based in Florida, posted on the Bitcointalk forum offering 10,000 BTC to anyone who would order, collect, and deliver two pizzas to him. A British user known as "Jercos" took him up on the offer, purchasing the pizzas for approximately $25 using a credit card, while receiving the 10,000 BTC in return.

"I'll pay 10,000 bitcoins for a couple of pizzas.. like maybe 2 large ones so I have some left over for the next day."
- Laszlo Hanyecz, Bitcointalk Forum (May 18, 2010)

Historical Significance

At the time, the 10,000 BTC was valued at roughly $41. This event is widely considered the crucial pivot point where Bitcoin transitioned from a theoretical cryptographic experiment into a functional medium of exchange. It proved that decentralized digital scarcity could hold tangible value in the physical world.
